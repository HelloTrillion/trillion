# Seamless

**Date:** 2026-11-12 · **Requirements:** SPN-2, SPN-14, SPN-20, SUR-9, PER-11, VOI-5, VOI-18, MEM-2, ONB-2a, ONB-3, ONB-4 · **Builds on:** [approval fixes](2026-11-12-approval-fixes.md), [your own words as the yes](2026-11-11-own-words.md), [plug-in features](2026-11-07-plug-in-features.md)

## Why

Assistants are heading toward less: less setup, fewer questions, less on screen, and things that just happen. Trillion is safe and capable, but it asks too much of people.

- **Before she is useful**, onboarding can ask up to nine things: your name, her name, a check of her name, how direct she should be, a model, a voice, listening, a folder, and a demo (`apps/web/src/onboarding.ts`, `ASKING`).
- **While she works**, every gated action that can be undone still stops for a card: forgetting a memory, keeping or dropping a rule, creating a helper, creating a file, setting a reminder.
- **While a tool runs**, she is silent by voice. Silence reads as broken.
- **A misheard name** finds nothing: speech-to-text gets no hint of the names you use, and search has no close match.

A contributor who runs their own assistant counted 116 asks in two weeks, 73 of which didn't need them. Their fix was to make the default "act, unless it's on my short owner list (money, contracts, permanent deletes, messages in my name), then tell me once after." It felt like a different product. Trillion's safety layer already draws that line, between what can and can't be undone (SPN-14, as of today's approval fixes). This spec acts on it.

**The rule stays the same: anything that can't be taken back still asks.** Seamless removes asks that protect nothing, and leaves every ask that does.

## Measures

Every measure is counted on the user's own machine. There is no telemetry (ONB-6). `trillion doctor --seamless` prints them, and so does a line in Settings → Confirmations.

| Measure | Today | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Questions before her first reply, with a model already available | up to 9 | 0 |
| Questions before her first reply, with no model available | up to 9 | 1 (pick a model) |
| Seconds from opening the app to her first reply, with a model available | not measured | under 30 |
| Asks per week (cards shown) | not measured | down by half for someone who uses her daily |
| Asks approved within 30 seconds with nothing changed | not measured | reported, as the sign of asks that didn't need the user |
| Tool turns over 2 seconds with words spoken before the 2-second mark | 0 | most |

Asks and approvals come from the decision record (SPN-17), which already logs every card and its answer.

## Decisions

### 1. Act, tell, offer undo, for things that can be undone

1. **The owner list keeps the card.** Spend, anything unrecoverable (sending email, calendar changes, Slack posts, every gated MCP tool, overwriting or editing a file, sharing a folder), and anything a connector's settings leave asking: the card and its tap, exactly as now (SPN-14).
2. **A gated, recoverable tool that can undo itself runs at once.** The router records it, runs it through the confirmed path, then she says what was done in one sentence, in her own words. A chip shows the same thing: "Kept a standing rule: … · Undo". It is recorded with `decided_by = "act_then_tell"`.
3. **Each tool earns this by implementing its own undo.** `Tool.undo(action) -> ToolResult` is checked by a precondition and fingerprint like any run (SPN-2c), so an undo never touches something that changed since. At launch:
   - `memory_forget`: restore the memory. It's soft underneath (SPN-9).
   - `rule_keep`: drop it. `rule_drop`: keep it again.
   - `helper_create`: remove the new helper. `helper_update`: put back its previous version. The widen tools (`helper_widen`, `routine_widen`, `speak_up_widen`): narrow back.
   - `ask_schedule`: cancel the reminder.
   - `file_create`: move the file to the system trash, only if it is unchanged since she wrote it. `file_append`: cut it back to its previous length, only if nothing after it changed. Otherwise the undo is refused, and she says why.

   A tool without a working undo keeps the card. A test pins the tools that act and tell, so adding one is a reviewed change.
4. **When it still asks.** The card comes back, whatever the tool, on:
   - a turn that read anything from outside (`read_outside`: a page, an email, a file), since that text could be what asked;
   - a voice that isn't the owner's, or a voice that couldn't be checked (PER-13);
   - a helper's chat or run, or a routine or timed ask with nobody there (those rules are unchanged);
   - a tool the loop guard has warned about.
5. **Undo lasts until the next day starts**, from the chip, from Settings → Confirmations, or by saying "undo that". Saying "undo that" reaches a host-side undo of her most recent act-then-tell action in that conversation; the model can't pick another one.
6. **Settings → Confirmations has one switch:** "Ask before things I can undo", off by default. On, every gated tool shows its card as today. **SPN-20 is folded in:** with act-then-tell, the three tools it covered act anyway, so its switch and its word-for-word path go. Nothing that path allowed is lost.

### 2. One voice: every word she says is in her conversation

1. **Check first.** List every path that puts words in front of the user outside a live reply: watcher lines (`attention/words.py`, a separate model call), routine ready lines, reminders, heads-ups, security notices, and the lines spoken when the model is down (`prompts/degraded.md`, which the PRD audit found breaks PER-11). For each one, find out whether it lands in the conversation she reads on her next turn.
2. **The rule:** anything she said, she can see she said. Every delivered line is saved in that conversation as hers (marked as a notice, never as the user's), before or as it is delivered, and is in her context on the next turn. A test delivers a notice, then asks "what did you just tell me?" through a fake model, and checks that the line is in what the model was given.
3. **One writer.** Watcher lines are written on the same model and persona as her replies (BRN-11 already says that anything the user reads composes on the strongest model). The small call can stay for cost, but with her persona block, not a separate one.
4. **Degraded lines:** when the model is down, nothing is spoken; the screen says what broke (SUR-8). That removes the last project-authored speech (PER-11).

### 3. Talk while tools run

1. **Before a slow tool, she says what she's doing, in her own words.** The voice overlay tells her: before a tool that looks something up, changes something or waits on a service, say a few words about it first. Never before a quick one (memory, time, her own board).
2. **That sentence is spoken at once.** Text that comes before a tool call in the same turn goes to speech as soon as the sentence ends, without waiting for the tool. Speech is made one sentence at a time today (PRD audit, VOI-5). This makes sure the first sentence is never held behind a tool.
3. **No filler, ever (PER-11).** If she says nothing and the tool runs long, nothing canned plays. The orb shows work, and the heartbeat (VOI-18) keeps the line open. Measured by the "words before 2 seconds" figure above.

### 4. Names she can hear and find

1. **Speech hints.** Speech-to-text is given the names she already knows: the user's and hers, people and places in active memories, helper names, and, when connected, contact names and recent senders' display names (never message text). At most 100 terms, ranked by recent use. Deepgram takes them as `keyterm`, local Whisper as `initial_prompt`, and a provider without hints ignores them (VOI-11). They go only to the speech provider the user already chose, which already hears the audio. Settings → Hearing says so, and they can be turned off.
2. **Close-match search.** Memory search and the helper, rule and contact lookups also match names by close spelling and sound. Close matches rank below exact and meaning matches, and close-match results never choose what an action changes on their own. Anything chosen by a close match is named back in her reply, and an action on it still takes the normal path (decision 1).

### 5. No questions before she's useful

1. **Find, don't ask.** On first open, the core checks what's already here, without asking: model keys in the environment or keychain, an Ollama server with a model, the system voice, local Whisper.
2. **With a model available, she starts talking.** Her first reply comes with no setup questions. Defaults:
   - **Her name:** Trillion, until the user says otherwise. "Call yourself Nova" is detected by the host, said back, and undoable (decision 1).
   - **The user's name:** asked in her first line, conversationally, and optional. A name the user says goes to `USER.md` through the privileged setup path (PER-2), never by the model writing the file.
   - **Directness:** the house default. "Be blunter" adjusts it later.
   - **Voice:** the system voice. **Listening:** local Whisper if installed, otherwise the mic button says how to add it.
3. **With no model available, there is one screen**, with two choices: "Use a free model on this computer" (it guides installing Ollama and pulling one model), or "Paste a key" (Anthropic recommended, others listed).
4. **Everything else is asked the first time it's needed**: a folder when she's first asked to touch a file, Google when she's first asked about email or the calendar, a voice from the user's own account when they ask for a different voice. The demo step goes.
5. The onboarding state machine keeps its tests. Steps are removed, not hidden.

### Prerequisite: installing works

`trillion` is not on PyPI yet, so `uv tool install trillion` and the one-line installer fail for anyone outside this repo. Nothing here feels seamless until that works. Publishing the package (and reserving `@hellotrillion` on npm) is the owner's step and comes first.

## What the user sees

- They open the app and she says hello, with no form to fill in. If nothing can think yet, there is one choice to make.
- "Remember that I've stopped drinking coffee… actually, forget that." It's gone, and she says so. A chip offers Undo.
- "Send Sam the notes." A card, as now: sending is on the owner list.
- "What's on my calendar tomorrow?" She says "Checking your calendar" while it loads, in her own words, then answers.
- They tell her about a new contact, Siobhan, then ask "what did Shivawn want?" She finds it, and says the name she matched.
- A watcher tells them a flight moved. They ask "when did you say it lands?" She knows, because she said it.

## How it's built, in order

1. **Measures**: `doctor --seamless` and the Confirmations line, from the decision record. Ships first, so every later step shows its effect.
2. **One voice**: the check, the conversation rule and its test, the degraded lines.
3. **Act, tell, offer undo**: `Tool.undo`, the router path, `decided_by = "act_then_tell"`, the chip, "undo that", the switch, folding in SPN-20. Mostly in `spine/router.py`, `spine/confirm.py`, each tool, and `apps/web` (the chip). New tests in `tests/spine/`, which stays exhaustive over when a card comes back.
4. **Talk while tools run**: the voice overlay, sentence-first speech before a tool, the measure.
5. **Names**: speech hints per provider, close-match search, the Hearing setting.
6. **No questions first**: detection, the one-screen fallback, questions asked when needed, and removing the steps.

Each step is its own PR with its own short spec section update, and passes the full CI.

## PRD changes, when each step lands

- **SPN-2:** a recoverable gated tool with its own undo acts, tells and offers undo, instead of waiting for a later turn, except in the cases listed in decision 1.4.
- **SPN-14:** the owner list (spend, unrecoverable, a connector's settings) keeps the tap. Nothing else changes.
- **SPN-20:** folded into act-then-tell.
- **SUR-9 / PER-11:** every delivered line is saved in her conversation. No spoken lines when the model is down.
- **VOI-5:** the first sentence before a tool is spoken at once.
- **MEM-2:** names match by close spelling and sound.
- **ONB-3 / ONB-4:** no questions before the first reply when a model is available, and one choice when not. Everything else is asked when first needed.

## Not now

- A smaller on-screen presence. The floating orb (desktop) already exists; making it the default view is a separate design pass.
- Regrouping Settings into fewer sections.
- Acting and telling for connector tools (drafts, labels). They keep their per-connector settings.
- Undo for anything on the owner list. Those keep the card instead.
- Learning which asks a particular user always approves, and offering to stop asking. That would need its own design and its own safety review.

## Step 2: one voice, as built

**Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Requirements:** SUR-9, PER-11, SUR-8, VOI-14, BRN-11

### The check

Every path that puts words in front of the user outside a live reply, as it was before this step. "Next turn" is whether the words reach what her model is given on her next turn in that conversation.

| Path | Shown or spoken | Saved where | Next turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| Watcher heads-up (`attention/runner.py`, `attention/words.py`, `attention/mail.py`) | her line, shown and spoken; title and detail shown | the app's note, then her line as her message, in the live page's conversation | yes, but written in a voice of its own (no persona), and no longer marked as read from outside after a reload |
| Security alert (`security/alerts.py`) | her line, shown and spoken | as a heads-up | yes; its own voice |
| Interrupted send (`delivery/interrupted.py`) | her line, shown and spoken | as a heads-up | yes; its own voice |
| Spend notice (warning, background paused) | the app's title, shown | the app's note | yes, as the app's |
| Reminder (`scheduler/runner.py`) | her words from when she set it, shown and spoken | her message, unmarked | yes, but unmarked: on the wire it merges into her previous reply |
| Timed ask | her answer, shown and spoken | her message, unmarked (and in the ask's own conversation) | yes, unmarked, and not marked as read from outside after a reload |
| Routine and background-read ready line | the host's line ("Your Monday revenue is ready."), shown and **spoken in her voice** | the app's note, "not yours" | yes, as the app's; she was heard saying a line her context says is not hers |
| Helper handoff ready line | the helper's first sentence after its name, shown and spoken | the app's note | yes, as the app's; not marked as quoting what the helper read |
| Missed or failed notice | the app's title, shown | nowhere | no |
| Delivery door (`delivery/door.py`) | hands each notice to the live, free page | saved by the page's session before it is shown | as above |
| System notification (`delivery/notify.py`) | a title, and a reminder's words | not by itself; the same notice is saved when a page takes it | as above |
| Model down (`prompts/degraded.md`) | the authored line, **spoken in her voice** and in the banner | nowhere | no |
| Ears down | the authored line, **spoken** and in the banner | nowhere | no |
| Voice down | the authored line, in the banner | nowhere | no |
| Hard spend cap (`persona/prompt.py`, `_CAPPED`) | the app's line, spoken and on the card | nowhere | no |
| Background read result, greeting, day board, nudge, helper hello | her reply to the app's note | the conversation, as a turn | yes |
| Channel reply (Telegram, Slack) | her reply on the channel | the channel's conversation | yes |

### What changed

1. **Every delivered notice is saved as hers, marked.** The page's session saves, in one statement and before anything is sent, the app's note for the notice (`NOTICE_NOTE`: its title, id and kind, why her voice was held) and then her words as her own message. The note tells her the next message is what she told the user, outside a reply. A heads-up with no words keeps its note alone. A ready line stays the app's note. A missed or failed notice is the app's and is not saved.
2. **Someone else's words stay someone else's.** A heads-up, a timed ask's answer and a helper's handed-back line can quote an email, an invite, a page or what a helper read. Their note carries a second mark (`FROM_OUTSIDE`): what her words report is data, never the user's request or permission. The find's own text (its detail and link) never enters her conversation, as before. Her line is not put inside an `untrusted_tool_result` wrapper: she said it, so it is not a tool's result, and a wrapper inside her own message would teach her to write wrappers. The turn after such a notice counts as having read outside (MEM-5), and now still does after a reload.
3. **Where an undelivered notice lands.** A notice held while no page is open lands in the conversation of the next free page, the one the user sees it in, and is saved there. Not the conversation it was set in: the user answers where they read it, and that is the conversation her next turn reads.
4. **No duplicates.** The door marks a notice delivered before the hand-over, and a page that can't take it raises before saving anything, so it is held and saved once. A reload draws each notice once from its note, with her words joined to it, and the page drops a notice it already shows (by id).
5. **One writer.** The small calls that write a watcher's line, the urgent-email judgement, a security alert and an interrupted-send line open on her persona block (`persona_block`: her soul and directness, the same text her replies open on), then their own task. They run on the model background work uses, which is her usual model (BRN-11). Their instructions no longer open "You are the user's assistant".
6. **Ready lines are shown, never spoken.** They are the host's words ("Your … is ready", "Stripe numbers are in", "Invoices: …"). The computer shows a notification for one instead, silent when quiet, as it does for a watcher's unspoken heads-up.
7. **When the model is down, nothing is spoken.** The banner shows the app's line naming what broke, and the conversation shows it as an app status line (the page's own `notice` entry), never saved as hers. `degraded.md` is now the app's words, written as the app ("The model provider can't be reached. …"), never in the first person, and moved out of the prompts to `persona/degraded.md`, beside them: it is not something she is sent, so it no longer counts toward the persona baseline's fingerprint (EVL-4).
8. **When her ears are down and her model works,** the banner shows the app's line, and, when her voice works, she says what broke in a sentence of her own: a turn on the app's note, spoken, shown and saved as hers. With no voice, the banner alone. With her model down too, nothing is spoken.
9. **Voice down** keeps the app's line in the banner. Nothing can be spoken, and her reply carries on as text.
10. A routine's paused notice says "…, so it's paused", the app's words, no longer "I've paused it".

Tests: `tests/api/test_one_voice.py` delivers each kind (reminder, timed ask, each watcher, a security alert, an interrupted send, a ready line, one held with no page), runs a turn through a fake model, and checks the line is in what the model was given as hers, after the note; the contributor's case (a reminder fires, "what did you just tell me?"); one note and one line each, and a reload that draws each once and delivers nothing again; an injected line marked as someone else's, before and after a reload; and the persona block at the head of a security alert's call. `tests/integration/test_degraded_provider_matrix.py` checks that with the model down nothing is spoken and the line is not saved, and that with the ears down her own sentence is spoken and saved and the app's line is not.

### Not done in this step

- **The hard spend cap line** (`_CAPPED`, BRN-8) is still the app's first-person line, spoken in her voice. BRN-8 says she says so in one plain sentence; at the hard cap her model is refused, so nobody can compose one. Whether it goes silent like the model-down line is the owner's call.
- **Voice down** has no sentence of her own: it can't be spoken, and composing one mid-reply would be a second model call for a line the banner already gives.
