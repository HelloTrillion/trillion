# Your day

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Requirements:** SUR-1, ONB-3 (host-authored turns), SPN-5 · **Builds on:** `docs/design/widgets.md` (the design system, packs and live widgets)

Ask "what's my day?", or tap **Your day** at the top of the thread list, and she
builds today's board from what you've connected: next up, free time, inbox,
mentions, what's due, yesterday's numbers, anything failing, and her own reminders.
She says what matters in two sentences. Because she makes the reads herself, every
card is live while you look.

## Decisions

| | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **A fresh day conversation each day**, `kind = "day"`, one per date, titled "Your day · Mon 26 Oct". | What the user chose: talk about the day in one place; past days stay in the thread list. |
| D2 | **Only when asked**: the "Your day" entry or her `day_open` tool. Nothing runs by itself. | What the user chose. |
| D3 | **She builds it with her normal tools, from a host-written recipe.** | Works for every connector; her reads make the cards live. |
| D4 | **The recipe is host-composed text, never the page's.** The page only asks (`day.build`), as it does for the greeting. | ONB-3: what she is told at the start of a turn is authored by the host. |
| D5 | **Personalised through her standing rules.** The recipe tells her to follow her rules for the day board. | No new settings to learn. |

## The day conversation

- Migration `012_day_conversations.sql`: `conversations.kind TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'chat'` and `conversations.day TEXT` (local date `YYYY-MM-DD`, in the user's zone), with a unique index on `(owner_id, day)` where `kind = 'day'` and it is not deleted (deleting today's gives a fresh one), and indexes on `usage` and `turn_timings` by conversation so the rebuild is fast.
- `GET /api/conversations/day`: today's day conversation for the user's zone, created when missing (title "Your day · Mon 26 Oct", `title_source` set so naming never renames it). Answers `{id, created}`.
- The conversations list (`GET /api/conversations`) carries `kind` and `day`.

## Starting it

- **The entry.** The thread list shows a "Your day" row above the threads, with today's date. Tapping it calls `GET /api/conversations/day`, switches to that conversation, and, when the board is empty (created now, or created earlier with nothing built), sends `day.build` once the session is up.
- **By asking.** A new tool `day_open` (Risk.READ, category `workspace`, no arguments): from a chat conversation it tells the page `day.open`, and the page does exactly what the entry does; her result is "Opening your day." Inside a day conversation, she rebuilds the board with her tools instead (the prompt says so), and `day_open` answers "This is your day already; rebuild the board here."
- **`day.build`.** The session accepts it only in today's day conversation, only when no turn is running, and at most once a minute. It queues the host-composed recipe as the turn's input, like the greeting does, and starts the turn. The recipe is saved as a host message (shown in the conversation as a small "Building your day" line, not as the user's words).

## The recipe

Composed by `persona/day.py` from what's set up and turned on (catalogue ids from MCP configs; Google services with an account; Slack connected), the user's zone and today's date:

| Source | What she reads and shows |
|---|---|
| Google Calendar | today's events → `next_up` (the next one) and `free_time` (today) |
| Gmail | unread and needing a reply → `inbox` |
| Slack | mentions since yesterday → `mentions` |
| Work pack (Linear, Jira, Todoist, ClickUp, monday.com, Notion, Airtable) | due today and tomorrow → `due_soon` |
| Money pack (Stripe, PayPal, Square) | yesterday against the day before → `kpis` |
| Building pack (GitHub, Sentry, Vercel, Supabase) | only if something is failing → `checks` or `errors` |
| Her own world (always) | today's reminders and timed asks (`schedule_list`) → `tasks` or `due_soon` |

The recipe's instructions: read first, then one `workspace_show` (mode `replace`, title "Your day"), at most 8 widgets, the most urgent first; name each source; skip a row whose read fails or returns nothing (no empty cards); follow your standing rules for the day board; then say at most two short sentences that lead with what matters most, and never read the board out. With nothing connected, she shows her reminders and a `callout` ("Connect your calendar and inbox to see your day here.") with an ask "How do I connect my calendar?".

## Prompt

One line in `text.md` and `voice.md` (no em or en dashes): when asked about the day ("what's my day", "brief me"), call `day_open`, unless this is already the day conversation, where you rebuild the board.

## Out of scope

- Building it by itself on open or on a schedule (routines, later).
- A permanent home board outside conversations.
- Server-built widgets without her.

## Testing

- **Server:**
  - the migration and one day conversation per date and zone;
  - `GET /api/conversations/day` creates once and then returns the same id;
  - recipe rows for: nothing connected, calendar only, everything;
  - `day.build` refused outside a day conversation, while a turn runs, and twice within a minute;
  - the recipe is saved as a host message and never as the user's;
  - `day_open` from a chat sends `day.open`, and inside a day conversation answers as specified;
  - the prompt lines are present.
- **Web:**
  - the "Your day" row;
  - tapping it opens, or creates and builds;
  - `day.open` switches and builds;
  - `day.build` is sent only for an empty board;
  - the host line is shown as "Building your day".
- **Live** (the user's Claude preview): "what's my day?" from a chat opens today's day conversation and builds a live board from the connected services; tapping "Your day" again opens it without rebuilding.
