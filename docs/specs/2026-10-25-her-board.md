# Her board: the workspace as how she shows things

**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Requirements:** SUR-1 (workspace), SUR-6, VOI-6 · **Respects:** SPN-5 (untrusted text), D5 of the reminders spec (unattended runs), never-list #11 (one writer), PER-5 (volatile context) · **Builds on:** `docs/specs/2026-10-24-sidebar-layout.md`

She can already talk. This lets her **show**. The user asks "show me X" or "take
the weather off" or "graph Z", and she puts widgets on a board in the workspace,
changes them, or removes them. She also opens it on her own when a picture says it
better than words. The board is her visual channel to the user, the way the orb is
her voice's face.

## Decisions

| | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | A **widget kit**: she picks typed widgets and fills them with data; the page draws them. No HTML from the model. | What the user chose. Always on-brand, can't break the page, and nothing she read can inject code. |
| D2 | She opens the board **when it helps**, not only when asked. She still says one short line; she never reads the board aloud. | What the user chose. |
| D3 | The board **belongs to its conversation** and is saved. Reopening the conversation brings it back, and she is told each turn what is on it. | What the user chose. |
| D4 | The tools are `Risk.READ` in a new category `workspace`. They never ask, and they are **never offered** to a run nobody is watching (scheduled asks) or to a channel. | Showing changes nothing in the world. With no page to show it on, a board would be invisible. |
| D5 | Every widget is **validated on the server** against the kit and its limits. A bad call is refused with a reason she can act on. | One place decides what a board may hold; the page trusts only that. |
| D6 | The page draws text with `textContent` only. No links, images or markup in this slice. | SPN-5: words she read on a page may end up on the board. |
| D7 | What the user does on the board (close it, remove a widget) **changes the saved board**, so she sees it on her next turn. | So she never refers to something the user took down. |

## What the user sees

- She says "Here's October" and the workspace opens with a board titled *October spend*: a stat, a line chart and a list. The orb docks in the corner as it does for Settings.
- "Take the list off": the list goes, the others close up. "Make it a bar chart": the chart changes in place.
- "Show me Saturday's options side by side": a compare widget replaces what was there.
- Each widget shows a small × on hover (always on touch). Removing one tells her.
- Closing the board (its close button, Escape, the docked orb, or "put that away") puts the orb back. She can open it again: the board is kept.
- The sidebar's **Workspace button** opens the current board. With none yet, an empty state reads "Ask her to show you something" and links to the sample preview (`#showcase`).
- Switching conversations shows that conversation's board, or none. Reloading brings the board back as it was, open or closed.

## The board

```json
{
  "title": "October spend",
  "open": true,
  "widgets": [
    { "id": "w1", "type": "stat", "size": "small", "label": "Total", "value": "$41.20", "delta": "+12%", "hint": "vs September" },
    { "id": "w2", "type": "chart", "size": "wide", "title": "Daily spend", "kind": "line",
      "x": ["Oct 1", "Oct 2"], "series": [{ "name": "Spend", "values": [1.2, 3.4] }], "unit": "$" }
  ]
}
```

- Ids are `w1`, `w2`, … given by the server, never reused within a board. They are how she refers to a widget.
- `size` is `small | medium | wide | full`. Default: `medium`. On a narrow workspace everything stacks.
- A board holds at most **12** widgets. Strings are trimmed and capped (titles 80, labels 60, values 40, other text 400 characters, unless noted).

### The kit (first slice)

| type | fields | limits |
|---|---|---|
| `stat` | `label`, `value`, `delta?`, `hint?`, `tone?` (`up\|down\|neutral`) | |
| `chart` | `title?`, `kind` (`line\|bar\|area\|donut`), `x` (labels), `series[]` (`name`, `values[]`), `unit?` | ≤5 series, ≤200 points; every series as long as `x`; values finite numbers; donut takes one series |
| `list` | `title?`, `items[]` (`title`, `subtitle?`, `meta?`, `done?`) | ≤30 items |
| `table` | `title?`, `columns[]`, `rows[][]` | ≤8 columns, ≤50 rows; every row as long as `columns`; cells strings or numbers |
| `note` | `title?`, `text` | ≤2000 characters; blank-line paragraphs, `- ` bullets and `**bold**` only |
| `timeline` | `title?`, `events[]` (`title`, `start`, `end?`, `meta?`) | ≤20 events; times `HH:MM` on one day |
| `compare` | `title?`, `options[]` (`name`, `note?`, `metrics[]` (`label`, `score` 0–10), `tags[]?`), `pick?` (an option's name), `reason?` | 2–4 options, ≤5 metrics each |

## Tools (`trillion/tools/workspace.py`)

All are `Risk.READ`, category `workspace`, `trusted_output=False` (their result quotes titles and labels she wrote, which may come from a page she read, so it is wrapped as untrusted like any other), timeout 5 s.

- `workspace_show(title?, widgets[], mode = "replace" | "add")`: puts widgets up and opens the board. `replace` clears what was there; `add` appends (12 at most). Result: `Shown on the board: w3 line chart "Daily spend", w4 list.`
- `workspace_update(id, widget)`: replaces one widget's content in place, keeping its id and position. Result names it.
- `workspace_remove(ids[])`: takes widgets off; `["all"]` takes everything off. Removing the last one closes the board.
- `workspace_close()`: closes the board and keeps it. `workspace_show` with no widgets reopens it as it was.

A refused call returns `ok=False` with the first problem in plain words, for example "chart w2: series 'Spend' has 12 values but x has 10." She can correct it and call again.

## Pieces

### Storage

```sql
CREATE TABLE boards (
    conversation_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES conversations(id),
    owner_id        TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES owners(id),
    board_json      TEXT NOT NULL,
    updated_at      INTEGER NOT NULL
);
```

`SqliteBoardStore` is the only writer: `get(conversation_id, owner_id) -> dict | None`, `put(conversation_id, owner_id, board, now_ms)`. `check_one_writer.py` lists `boards.board_json` and `boards.updated_at`. A deleted conversation's board stays with it (soft delete).

### Service

`Boards(store, sessions)` with `show`, `update`, `remove`, `close`, `user_close`, `user_remove`, `current`. Each one loads the board, applies the change through `board.py` (pure, tested), saves it, and sends `board{board}` to every page open on that conversation. Ids come from a counter kept in the board (`next_id`).

### Session and context

- After `history`, `Session.start` sends `board{board}` (null when there is none).
- Page messages: `board.close` and `board.remove{id}` go through the service, so the saved board and her context follow the user.
- `volatile_context` gains `board`: one line, for example `Board (open): "October spend": w1 stat "Total", w2 line chart "Daily spend".` Titles and labels in it are clipped to 40 characters and the line is wrapped as untrusted (it may quote what she read). With no board, nothing is added.

### Prompt

`text.md` and `voice.md` get a short rule: use the board for numbers, comparisons, plans and anything with more than three items; put it up with the workspace tools; say one short line about it and never read it out; keep it tidy (update or remove instead of piling up); refer to widgets by what they show, not by id, when talking.

### Web

- `chart.ts` (pure, tested): scales, nice ticks, line/area paths, bar rects, donut arcs.
- `board.ts`: draws a board into the workspace (`workspace.open(title, el, 'board')`) as a responsive grid (container queries), one glass card per widget, × on each, and an empty state. It redraws on each `board` message, keeping scroll position, and animates only widgets that are new.
- The Workspace button opens the board, or the empty state.
- Closing the workspace while it shows the board sends `board.close`. A board arriving with `open: true` opens the workspace unless Settings is open; then it waits behind a small "She put something on the board" chip in the sidebar.
- Phones: one column; the sheet collapses as it does for Settings.

## Errors

- A refused tool call: she gets the reason and the board does not change.
- Saving fails: the tool returns `ok=False` ("the board could not be saved") and the page does not change.
- A page message for an unknown widget id is ignored.

## Out of scope

- New data sources. A chart is only as good as what she can already read.
- Approval cards on the board (they stay in the conversation).
- Free-form HTML, images and links.
- Several boards per conversation, or a history of boards.
