The app's line when one leg is down (VOI-14, SUR-8): one sentence each, naming what
broke and what happens next. These are the app's words, not hers: shown in the banner,
and in the conversation as an app status, never spoken and never saved as her message
(PER-11, the seamless spec, decision 2). When she can still think and speak (her ears

model.unauthorized: The model provider turned down the key. Check it in Model settings.
model.forbidden: The model provider says this key isn't allowed to do that. Check it in Model settings.
model.payment_required: The model provider says the account is out of credit.
model.rate_limited: The model provider is limiting requests. Give it a minute and ask again.
model.unreachable: The model provider can't be reached. Check the connection, or switch to a model on this computer.
model.timeout: The model provider stopped answering. Ask again in a moment.
model.not_found: The model provider doesn't know that model. Pick another in Model settings.
model.provider_unavailable: The model provider is having trouble. Ask again in a few minutes.
model.other: The model provider failed. Ask again, or check Model settings.
hearing.inaudible: No sound is coming from the microphone. Check it isn't muted, or type instead.
hearing.unauthorized: The listening provider turned down the key. Typing still works.
hearing.payment_required: The listening account is out of credit. Typing still works.
hearing.rate_limited: The listening provider is limiting requests. Typing still works.
hearing.unreachable: The listening provider can't be reached. Typing still works.
hearing.timeout: The listening provider stopped answering. Typing still works.
hearing.provider_unavailable: The listening provider is having trouble. Typing still works.
hearing.not_installed: Listening on this computer isn't installed. Mic settings has the fix, and typing still works.
hearing.needs_download: The listening model hasn't been downloaded yet. Mic settings has it, and typing still works.
hearing.other: Listening isn't working right now. Typing still works.
voice.unauthorized: The voice provider turned down the key. Answers come in text.
voice.payment_required: The voice account is out of credit. Answers come in text.
voice.rate_limited: The voice provider is limiting requests. Answers come in text.
voice.unreachable: The voice provider can't be reached. Answers come in text.
voice.timeout: The voice provider stopped answering. Answers come in text.
voice.provider_unavailable: The voice provider is having trouble. Answers come in text.
voice.other: The voice isn't working right now. Answers come in text.
